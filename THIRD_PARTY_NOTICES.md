Firmware C/ASM, ELF/maps, reverse-engineering material, vendor-key/decryption tools and full private device dumps are not part of the public tree. The SD package binaries are preserved byte for byte from the hardware-tested private-profile build; embedded compatibility hashes are intentionally retained.
